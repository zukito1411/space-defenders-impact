Immersion presentation pack (audio/immersion/, November 2026)

- Protection, destruction, engine, environment, deployment and carrier foley:
  layered, filtered, time-edited derivatives of the CC0 Mesiah deck/radio and
  boss recordings credited above. No additional downloaded recordings.
- score-cruise, score-amber, score-silent, score-violet, score-command,
  score-squid, score-snake and score-final: percussive stems separated from
  the respective bundled scores. All original music attribution and license
  obligations listed above continue to apply, including Kevin MacLeod's
  Space Fighter Loop (CC BY 4.0) for score-command.
- pa-clear-lane and pa-maintenance: original flight-deck dialogue, synthesized
  locally with Windows Microsoft Zira Desktop, then filtered and reverberated.
  Dialogue: "Flight deck. Keep the launch lane clear."
  "Maintenance team. Report to the aft service deck."
- manifest.json records each derived file's source recordings, duration,
  channel count, sample rate, byte length and SHA-256 digest.
