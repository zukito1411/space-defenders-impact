# Space Jet force field

The Space Jet passive force field uses a transparent generated shield sprite, drawn beneath the ship. The existing procedural effect remains the fallback while the sprite loads or if it fails.

Asset: `public/assets/raid-fx/space-jet-force-field-v1.webp`. Generated with the built-in image generator and resized to 512×256 with alpha preserved for game use.

Prompt: Isolated production game VFX sprite, on transparent background. A single thin electric turquoise and emerald energy shield outline: horizontal ellipse surrounding an EMPTY fully transparent center, with a few small glowing hexagon fragments and fine electric arcs on the outline only. The entire outer area must be fully transparent, no haze or white backdrop. No solid colored surfaces, no spacecraft, no weapons, no text, no frame, no floor, no smoke. Centered compact oval, generous transparent margin on every side. For a 2D top-down space game, luminous cyan/green rim and subtle alpha glow.
